## Specific risks and decisions before execution

- **Reach/size mismatch:** at preview scale 0.55, the sprite's peak tip is `(499−256)×0.55 = 133.65` world units from its pivot; engine range is 105. The authored resting base spans 237.6 world units, much wider than the old mechanics silhouette and its 25-unit center clamp near walls. Approve a renderer-only scale/anchor/edge-framing map before integration; a candidate attack scale is `105/243 ≈ 0.4321`, subject to the character-in-scene keyframe. Keep accepted source geometry and engine hitboxes unchanged. Do not silently accept visible hits beyond mechanical reach or change collision to fit art.
- **Impact phase:** engine damage resolves immediately on input; the 220 ms animation reaches peak extension later. Preserve the existing damage/cooldown timing. Review the resulting contact/reaction together; if it looks unacceptable, return to Offer for an approved presentation-phase/cue decision rather than delaying damage or editing the accepted attack schedule by inference.
- **Style/visibility:** current SVG/PNG assets can be integrated, but their clean finish, large body compression, static remaining forms, foreground occlusion and phone crops may fail the desired coherence. Keyframe approval and a real bay gate are mandatory; functional tests cannot settle these questions.
- **Runtime cost:** six full-width RGBA layers alone decode to about 24.6 MiB; 36 character frames add about 10.5 MiB, before GPU/browser copies. Crop layers with recorded offsets, preload once, avoid per-frame fetch/decode, and measure. No atlas, renderer replacement or paid tooling unless separately justified/approved.
